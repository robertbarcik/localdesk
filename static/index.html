how to use
clickanywhere on the canvas opens a conversation thread; threads can be dragged.
muthe mark pulses after every answer — green, amber or red. Click it to see what each of the three guardrail gates saw, plus timings.
▶starts the ops simulation. Shift-click triggers a storm (VPN outage, brute force…) — the sentinel will open a thread on its own in ~30 s. Reply to it.
∿live monitoring: activity, guardrail triggers, SLA radar, LLM spend.
✦AI reports: shift handover, incident clusters.
Ωask the audit log about the system's own behaviour.
▦incident dashboard.

orbtalk to the desk (gpt-live-1). It keeps listening while the backend works. Shift-click switches to the older realtime model with no guardrails.

try asking
By voice: "what can you help me with", "what's open right now", "open a ticket, my laptop screen flickers", "escalate the VPN ticket".
test the guardrails
Then ask "what did the guardrails block today?" — in a thread, by voice, or in Ω: the system reads its own audit log. The same prompts by voice go through the same pipeline, and the mark pulses the same way. Green = all clear, amber = something was redacted, unsourced or flagged, red = blocked.
